Explore Images Beyond Pixels.

We usually see an image as a grid of pixels. It can also be understood as a combination of spatial frequencies.

Change their magnitude, shift their phase, or filter the spectrum. Observe how an image comes back together—and connect an abstract idea to something you can see.

01 / Why Fourier Transform?

Pixels tell us what is at each position. A Fourier spectrum reveals patterns of variation: broad changes, fine detail, and repeated structures. Looking at both helps us ask which frequency components contribute to what we see.

MagnitudeThe amplitude of each spatial frequency component. It tells us how strongly that component contributes.

PhaseThe phase of each component. How these components align has an important influence on spatial structure in the reconstructed image.
